隐私政策

最后更新：2026-09-16 · 联系我们：privacy@ohmyterm.com

本政策说明 Oh My Term 官方服务（官方运营的服务器与相关网站）如何处理你的数据。Oh My Term 是一个远程终端工具：绝大多数情况下，你的数据只存在于你自己的设备和你连接的机器之间。

我们处理哪些数据

数据用途
账号信息：用户名、密码（仅以 Argon2id 哈希形式存储）、可选的两步验证（TOTP）、可选的第三方登录绑定（如 GitHub 的用户标识）注册、登录与账号安全
设备授权信息：你添加设备的名称、公钥、授权记录在客户端展示设备列表并控制哪些设备可以连接
会话列表元数据：会话名称、启动命令、工作目录等在多端之间同步你的会话列表
界面偏好设置（主题、排序等）在多端之间同步你的个性化设置
连接记录：连接的 IP 地址与时间安全防护（如登录失败限流）以及在设备管理界面展示最近活动

终端内容是端到端加密的

你在终端中输入和看到的内容（按键、输出）在你的浏览器/客户端与你的设备之间端到端加密传输。官方服务器只承担加密数据的转发，无法读取终端内容。

我们不做什么

  • 不出售你的数据，不与第三方共享用于广告或营销
  • App 内没有广告和第三方跟踪组件
  • 不请求与核心功能无关的系统权限（位置、通讯录等）

数据保留与删除

你可以通过应用内的账号删除入口删除账号，也可以按 账号删除说明 通过邮件请求删除。删除账号会一并删除其账号信息、设备授权、偏好设置与相关记录。

如果你使用的不是官方服务器

Oh My Term 客户端可以连接任何 omts 服务器（自建、组织部署或第三方运营的）。如果你登录使用的服务器地址不是官方服务的地址，上述数据由该服务器的运营者处理，本政策不适用——隐私与删除请求请直接联系你的服务提供方，本页联系方式不处理第三方服务器上的用户数据。

政策变更

如有重大变更，我们会在官方网站公布并更新本页面的「最后更新」日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-16 · Contact: privacy@ohmyterm.com

This policy explains how the official Oh My Term service (servers operated by Oh My Term and related websites) handles your data. Oh My Term is a remote terminal tool: in almost all cases your data stays between your own devices and the machines you connect to.

What we process

DataPurpose
Account info: username, password (stored only as an Argon2id hash), optional two-factor authentication (TOTP), optional third-party sign-in link (e.g. a GitHub user id)Registration, sign-in, account security
Device authorization: names, public keys and grant records of devices you addShowing your device list and controlling which devices may connect
Session list metadata: session names, start commands, working directoriesSyncing your session list across devices
UI preferences (theme, sort order, …)Syncing your settings across devices
Connection records: IP address and time of connectionsAbuse protection (e.g. sign-in rate limiting) and showing recent activity in device management

Terminal content is end-to-end encrypted

What you type and see in a terminal (keystrokes, output) travels end-to-end encrypted between your browser/client and your device. The official server only relays ciphertext and cannot read terminal content.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking components
  • We do not request system permissions unrelated to the core function (location, contacts, …)

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account through the in-app account deletion entry, or request deletion by email as described in the account deletion notice. Deleting an account removes its account info, device grants, preferences and related records.

If you use a non-official server

The Oh My Term client can connect to any omts server (self-hosted, organization-deployed, or run by a third party). If the server you sign in to is not operated by Oh My Term, the data described above is processed by that server's operator and this policy does not apply — direct privacy and deletion requests to your service provider. We do not handle user data on third-party servers.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced on the official website and reflected in the "last updated" date above.
