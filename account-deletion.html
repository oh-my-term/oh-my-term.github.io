删除你的 Oh My Term 账号

最后更新：2026-09-16 · 联系我们：privacy@ohmyterm.com

你可以随时删除 Oh My Term 官方服务的账号，无需重新安装应用。有两种方式：

方式一：在应用内删除

  1. 打开 Oh My Term 客户端并登录
  2. 进入「设置 → 安全 → 删除账号」
  3. 按提示完成身份确认，确认后即执行删除

方式二：通过邮件请求删除

  1. 向 privacy@ohmyterm.com 发送邮件，主题注明「删除账号」
  2. 正文中写明你的账号用户名（如忘记用户名，提供注册邮箱或绑定的第三方登录方式）
  3. 我们会在 7 天内 完成删除并回复确认

删除的内容

  • 账号信息（用户名、密码哈希、两步验证、第三方登录绑定）
  • 所有已授权设备的授权记录
  • 偏好设置、会话列表等与账号关联的数据

删除操作不可恢复。你连接的自有机器（设备上安装的常驻进程）不受影响，可在设备上自行卸载。

如果你使用的是自建服务器

自建服务器的账号数据由该服务器的运营者掌握，请直接联系你的运营者删除。

Delete your Oh My Term account

Last updated: 2026-09-16 · Contact: privacy@ohmyterm.com

You can delete your official Oh My Term service account at any time — no app reinstall required. Two ways:

Option 1: delete in the app

  1. Open the Oh My Term client and sign in
  2. Go to “Settings → Security → Delete account”
  3. Confirm your identity when prompted, then confirm to delete the account

Option 2: request deletion by email

  1. Email privacy@ohmyterm.com with the subject “Account deletion”
  2. Include your account username (or, if forgotten, your sign-up email or linked sign-in method)
  3. We complete the deletion and reply within 7 days

What gets deleted

  • Account info (username, password hash, two-factor settings, linked sign-ins)
  • Authorization records for all of your devices
  • Preferences, session lists and other account-linked data

Deletion is irreversible. Machines you connected (running the device agent) are unaffected — uninstall the agent on the device itself if you no longer want it.

If you use a self-hosted server

Accounts on a self-hosted server belong to that server's operator — please contact them to delete your data.
